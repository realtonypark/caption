To the people who still read closely,

Reading matters more than readers. A reader alone is passive. A companion is something you think with. You stay present and in control while the page is still alive.

Most tools ask you to move your thinking into their ecosystem and rules. Your reading should stay in software you control, with a record that works in your browser, on your shelf.

Interfaces change. What you understood should remain yours. Highlight it, question it, keep it — not in ten scattered places, but in one margin that remembers.

Caption is our attempt to build that reading companion.

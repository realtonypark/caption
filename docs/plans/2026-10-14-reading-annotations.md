# Reading annotations

The reader uses a floating selection toolbar with Ask, Explain, Define or Simplify, additional actions, four highlight colors, and a note editor. Typing on a selection begins a question. Notes save on Enter or an outside click; Escape cancels the draft. Existing highlights can be recolored, edited, or removed.

Desktop questions and personal notes sit beside their passages on a ruled-paper margin, without a visible margin title. Cards share a collision-free layout. Answers collapse to a question, or an action with its excerpt, and a gist; expanded cards support follow-ups, paragraph links, copying, retrying a sample response, and deletion. A whole-text question bar stays at the bottom of the article. On phones, selection actions dock below and active answers use a bottom sheet with controls to reach every saved answer.
